# --grill: walk an existing plan or idea

Grilling sharpens what is already written until every load-bearing decision has an explicit yes. It invents no options (that is `--options`) and writes no files while it runs.

1. **One question per message, asked last** on its own line: `**Ask:** <one question>?`. A stacked list ("1. ... 2. ...", or questions held for later) gets a polite yes, not a decision. Name the next topic without asking it.
2. **State your hypothesis first:** your best guess, your confidence, and the answer you recommend, then the question.
3. **Never ask what you can look up** in the record, the code or the docs.
4. **A yes is explicit** and on a concrete restatement. "Sounds good" is not a yes.
5. **Out of scope is part of the answer:** ask for it if nobody said it.
6. **Order by blast radius:** one-way doors and data shape first, naming last.

Done when no blocking decision is left without a recommended answer and a yes. Then write the answers into the record's `SPEC.md` (mini-requirements and contract) and `PROGRESS.md` (the decision and who made it), and stop.
