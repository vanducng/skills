# Data Platform Engineer (mid-level)

*Fictional JD for skill examples. Mid-size product company, hybrid HQ, mid-level band.*

## About the team

We are a mid-size B2B product company (~200 people). A small data group owns the warehouse, the pipelines that feed product and finance, and the quality checks those teams already page on. There is no separate 30-person platform org. Hybrid at HQ a few days a week; core hours overlap US daytime.

## The role

Mid-level Data Platform Engineer. You will:

- Design and operate ELT/ETL into a cloud warehouse (Snowflake today; equivalent warehouse experience is fine)
- Model data in dbt (or equivalent) that other people ship against
- Schedule and observe jobs (Airflow or similar)
- Use Python and SQL as daily tools
- Own enough AWS, IaC, and CI/CD to change a pipeline without a ticket to another team
- Wear more than one hat: a Streamlit/internal tool or a small API is normal here

## Must-haves

- Python and SQL used in production work
- About 3+ years of data, platform, or cloud engineering (analyst-only or BI-only is not a substitute)
- At least one cloud warehouse: Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks, or Synapse
- Comfortable in a small/fast product team (design → ship → on-call)

## Nice-to-haves

- dbt + Airflow (or Dagster/Prefect) in production
- AWS (Lambda, S3, IAM) and Terraform or similar
- Docker; Kubernetes is optional
- Internal apps (Streamlit/Flask) or warehouse AI/LLM features you actually shipped

## Logistics (HR screens these — do not turn them into interview questions)

- Hybrid, HQ metro, several days on site
- Mid-level IC band, about $80–100k depending on location and scope
- Core hours overlap US daytime

## How we read a resume

We score pipelines, core stack, cloud/platform, extras, band fit, location/hours (only what the resume states), and fact integrity. We do not treat missing public cert badges as a lie. We do treat a LinkedIn title/employer that contradicts the resume as a knockout.
