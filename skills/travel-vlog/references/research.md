# What makes a short travel video work on YouTube

Checked on 2026-11-25. Re-check the YouTube Help pages before relying on a number: they change without notice.

| Topic | Finding | What the skill does with it | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | The first 15 seconds make or break a video; state the value or preview the payoff at once. Studio's key-moments report shows the intro drop. | First shot is the best moment of the topic, with a hook line or a place title in the first 3 s. No logo, no "welcome back". | [YouTube Help: key moments for audience retention](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415); [Search Engine Land: audience retention](https://searchengineland.com/guide/audience-retention) |
| Retention | Retention rarely recovers what it loses in the first 30 s; chain small payoffs and change the picture often (B-roll, angle changes). | Shots of 2 to 5 s, one topic per video, 60 to 120 s. | [vidIQ: audience retention](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/increase-audience-retention-youtube) |
| Thumbnail spec | Custom thumbnails need a verified account (intermediate features). The page now recommends 16:9 at up to 3840x2160, at least 640 px wide, JPG or PNG, under 50 MB. The older 1280x720 under 2 MB still uploads. | 1280x720 JPEG under 2 MB: accepted everywhere and small. Check eligibility in Studio, Settings, Channel, Feature eligibility. | [YouTube Help: add custom thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431) |
| Thumbnail design | 90% of the best-performing videos use custom thumbnails; relatable emotion (a face), rule of thirds, simple design, easy font. | A face or one strong frame, contrast and saturation lifted, a dark side gradient for the words. | [YouTube Help: thumbnail and title tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300) |
| Thumbnail words | Keep text to about 3 to 5 big words in a bold sans; keep the lower-right corner clear (duration badge); the thumbnail should add to the title, not repeat it. | `thumb` refuses fewer than 2 or more than 4 words and sets them top-left or top-right. | [vidIQ: thumbnail design tips](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-thumbnail-design-tips); [Buffer: scroll-stopping thumbnails](https://buffer.com/resources/youtube-thumbnails) |
| Series look | One focal point, strong contrast, few words, the same style across a series so viewers know it is yours. | One layout and two colours for every thumbnail in a series. | [wthumb: thumbnail best practices 2026](https://wthumb.co/blog/youtube-thumbnail-best-practices) |
| Thumbnail metric | An impression counts when the thumbnail shows over 1 s with at least half visible; click-through in the first 24 h on Home and Suggested is the measure. | Check the phone-size preview: it is what most impressions look like. | [YouTube Help: impressions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314486) |
| Titles | Be accurate, put the most important words first, keep episode numbers and branding at the end; searchable titles reach search, intriguing ones reach browse. | Default title `<Series> · Day <d> · <Topic>`: fine for an Unlisted memory series shared by link. For a public video aimed at search, put the topic first and the series after it. | [YouTube Help: thumbnail and title tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300) |
| Playlists | A series lives in a playlist; titles up to 150 characters, descriptions up to 5,000; order by drag and drop. Playlist privacy can be Unlisted. | One playlist per trip, videos added in day and number order. | [YouTube Help: playlists](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57792); [YouTube Help: manage playlists in Studio](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10232933) |
| Shorts | A square or vertical video up to 3 minutes is a Short (uploads since 2024-10-15); 16:9 is never a Short; 1080x1920 is the practical export. | The default output is 16:9 1080p. A vertical cut is a separate edit list with `size: [1080, 1920]`. | [YouTube Help: get started with Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070); [YouTube Help: three-minute Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877) |
| Loudness | YouTube plays back at about -14 LUFS: louder uploads are turned down, quieter ones are not turned up. Peaks at or below -1 dBTP. | Two-pass `loudnorm` to -14 LUFS, true peak -1.5 dBTP; `verify` fails outside +/-1 LU. | [iZotope: mastering for streaming](https://www.izotope.com/community/blog/mastering-for-streaming-platforms); [Sound Design RV: LUFS per platform](https://www.sounddesignrv.com/en/insights/what-lufs-to-deliver-a-video-instagram-youtube-tv-cinema) |
| Music licence | Audio Library tracks under the YouTube licence need no credit; Creative Commons tracks must be credited in the description (Studio's copy button gives the text). The automatic "Music in this video" panel does not count as credit. | Each music entry records `licence` and `credit`; the credit goes in the description when it is not empty. | [YouTube Help: Audio Library](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882) |
